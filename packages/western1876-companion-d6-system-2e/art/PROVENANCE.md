# Official 1876 logo

`branding/1876-logo.png` is the unchanged transparent PNG supplied by Geir and
explicitly selected as the replacement logo through the established Design task
on 8 September 2026. Its Store-retained source is `1876-user-logo.png` in the
Design workstream's `transparent-logo` evidence directory.

The file is 1,536 × 1,024 pixels, 8-bit RGBA PNG, 1,530,879 bytes. Its SHA-256 is
`695d97a5d4729d43ab5ac3fadc8e06748de4c2cea82a930cb71a2419976a3ac4`.
The complete supplied image, including its alpha channel, decorative rules and
small **D** inset inside the final **6**, is retained byte-for-byte. No redraw,
conversion, background removal, cropping or recoloring was performed. Display
the complete aspect ratio as an image; do not substitute a mask.

The prior `branding/1876-logo.webp` remains available for existing saved references
but is no longer the companion's default logo. It was an unchanged copy of the
website's `website/public/art/ink-logo.webp`, derived through
`website/art-sources/ink-logo.png` from `Logo-Draft1.png`; its 5 September 2026
provenance is recorded in `docs/website/ART-PROMPTS.md` in the authoring project.
That older asset has an opaque black background.

This is the user-authorized replacement asset. No new artwork was generated or
imported from another game's setting. Source and packaged hashes are recorded
in the Legacy workstream's Store-backed evidence manifest. The font, palette,
component styles and rules are unchanged.

## Outlaw alternative

`branding/1876-outlaw-logo.png` is the separate red PNG supplied by Geir for an
alternative appearance, explicitly named **Outlaw** with a darker mood. The
existing gold PNG remains unchanged. The established Design task retained the
source as `theme-variants/1876-red-user-logo.png` on Store and confirmed the
five Outlaw palette tokens after normal and narrow offline review.

The Outlaw image is 1,536 × 1,024 pixels, 8-bit RGBA PNG, 1,297,759 bytes.
SHA-256: `abddb332612c57944ada57bd08149c0c9d9be6f0dc7ebe3032f5a0f836980d53`.
It is copied byte-for-byte, including the supplied alpha channel. No redraw,
conversion, cropping or recoloring occurred. Preserve its complete aspect ratio.

The Outlaw tokens are background `#090607`, text `#ead9d1`, accent `#f08c80`,
accentBright `#f3b0a3`, and muted `#baa69e`. Dark surfaces and the red mark provide
the mood; the lighter red text and interaction accents preserve contrast on
derived panels. The existing 1876 gold tokens are unchanged. The separate
Design-owned amendment extends the existing heading-fit scope to the Outlaw
Setting Profile; this companion introduces no CSS or new theme engine.
