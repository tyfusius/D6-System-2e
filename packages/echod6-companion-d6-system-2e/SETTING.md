# Echo

One moment you were living an ordinary life.

The next, you awaken in a place called Authority Arrival, where bewildered
newcomers from countless worlds are processed, questioned and given a choice:
adapt… or disappear.

Before long, you find yourself assigned to The Wayward—an aging traversal
vessel with a seasoned crew and a reputation for accepting missions others
refuse.

Your new life begins at sea.

Not upon water, but upon the Drift—a mysterious expanse lying between realities.
Guided by skilled Navigators, traversal vessels plunge ever deeper through
impossible storms in search of portals leading to other realms. Some worlds
hold forgotten civilizations, lost treasures and unimaginable discoveries.
Others hide predators, pirates, ancient ruins and mysteries that have remained
untouched for centuries.

No one knows why you were brought here.

Perhaps the answer lies within the strange Relics scattered throughout the
realms—alien artifacts of petrified living bone, each possessing abilities
beyond modern understanding. Perhaps it lies somewhere beyond the deepest
known routes, where few vessels have ever returned.

Echo is a campaign of exploration, mystery and high adventure.

Each expedition takes the crew into a new realm inspired by history, mythology,
pulp adventure and science fantasy. Between missions you’ll return to Bastion
Reach, upgrade The Wayward, build alliances, uncover conspiracies and slowly
piece together the truth behind your arrival.

The greatest discoveries aren’t just new worlds.

They’re the secrets waiting beyond the next horizon.
